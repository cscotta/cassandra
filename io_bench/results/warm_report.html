Caveats.
  • Page-cache hit rate is the reliable metric here (cachestat mm counters, cross-checked by the VFS-vs-device IOPS delta). The byte-level VFS-vs-device figure is confounded by 16 KB-page over-fetch and is not shown.
  • At this throughput, per-op eBPF read counts undercount (high-frequency tracepoint loss); use the cold isolated tool (cold_readbench.py) for exact per-request IO (2 reads, 72 KB/req). Ratios (hit rate) are robust; absolute per-op eBPF counts at scale are lower bounds.
  • disk_access_mode=standard (buffered index), chunk cache off, Zstd@8 64 KB chunks, LCS, data on raw XFS (/dev/nvme0n1p8). Working set ~188 GB > 62 GB RAM.
